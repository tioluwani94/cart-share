## Description

Grocery shopping is easier when the whole household remembers together. OurPantry brings your shared list, everyday regulars and grocery spending into one place.

ONE LIST, TOGETHER
Add what you need, check it off as you shop, and keep household members up to date. Create separate lists for other shops and occasions.

A QUICK KITCHEN CHECK
Review your regular products before the next shop. Swipe or tap to say what you need, what you still have, or what you are unsure about. You choose what goes on the list.

YOUR HOUSEHOLD’S PANTRY
Find the products you buy regularly, adjust reminder timing, and pause anything you no longer want to track. Completed shops help OurPantry learn possible regulars; they become reminders only when you choose to track them. This is a memory of what you buy, not a live count of what is left.

PLAN THE NEXT SHOP
Choose a date, time and shopping mode so your household knows the plan.

SEE YOUR GROCERY SPENDING
Set a monthly budget, enter a trip total or scan a receipt, and review recent trips and monthly spending. Receipt totals can be checked and edited before saving. The current version uses GBP for budgets and totals.

YOU STAY IN CONTROL
Notifications and usage analytics are optional. Manage your preferences or delete your account in Settings.
